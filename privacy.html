Privacy

Last updated · 24 September 2026

Your prompts

The prompt checker runs in your browser. Prompts entered into the checker are not sent to our server or stored by us.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate site usage, such as page visits and interactions. Clarity may use cookies or similar technologies under Microsoft's privacy terms. Do not enter personal or confidential information into the prompt field.

Local storage

The early-access interest button may save a simple status on your device so the interface can remember your choice. It does not collect payment or contact details.

External links

The site links to third-party documentation and services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Contact

If a contact channel is added, this page will be updated. Until then, do not submit personal data through the checker.
